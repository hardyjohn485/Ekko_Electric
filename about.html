ABOUT US

The top electrical panel board manufacturing company in Kottayam, Kerala.

ekko electric is the top electrical panel board manufacturing company in Kerala, based in Kottayam. We specialize in Low Tension (LT) panel board manufacturing, catering to industries, commercial establishments, and homes with precision and excellence. Since our inception, we have been dedicated to pioneering electrical solutions that meet and exceed the highest industry standards.

Our journey began with a vision to become the best electrical panel board manufacturing company in Kerala. Over the years, we have developed deep expertise in delivering home electrical panel board solutions as well as large-scale industrial control panels — earning the trust of clients across Kerala and beyond.

Our extensive product portfolio includes Power Control Centers (PCC), Motor Control Centers (MCC), Distribution Boards, Automatic Power Factor Correction (APFC) Panels, Automatic Mains Failure (AMF) Panels, and customized panels — all manufactured at our facility in Kottayam, Kerala.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred electrical panel board company in Kerala, known for quality, innovation, and customer-centric approach — and the go-to partner for LT panel board manufacturing in Kerala across all sectors.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver top-quality electrical panel board solutions that enhance safety, efficiency, and reliability. As a leading electrical panel board manufacturing company in Kottayam, we serve homes, industries, and commercial clients across Kerala with innovative designs and exceptional service.

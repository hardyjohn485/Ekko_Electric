About ekko electric

Welcome to ekko electric, a leading electrical panel board manufacturing company in Kerala, based in Kottayam. We design, manufacture, and supply standard and custom-built LT panel boards for a wide range of industries and residential projects.

Our commitment to quality, safety, and performance has established us as the best electrical panel board manufacturing company in Kerala. From home electrical panel board solutions to heavy-duty industrial control panels, we deliver every product engineered to the highest standards.

Core Manufacturing Domains

Comprehensive LT panel board manufacturing expertise serving Kottayam and all districts of Kerala.

Main Switch Boards (MSB) & PCC

High-capacity Power Control Centers engineered for major petroleum corporations (HPCL, IOCL, Nayara) and industrial facilities, featuring heavy-duty switchgear and multi-tiered fault protection.

  • Current ratings up to 4000A
  • High-conductivity copper & aluminum busbars
  • Form 3b / Form 4b modular compartmentalization

Motor Control Centers (MCC) & Automation

Specialized motor management panels with DOL, Star-Delta, soft starters, and Variable Frequency Drives (VFD) ensuring smooth motor starting and optimal energy consumption.

  • VFD & Soft-starter integration
  • PLC automated sequence controllers
  • Thermal overload & phase-reversal protection

Automatic Mains Failure (AMF) Panels

Mission-critical automatic power changeover systems for hospitals, datacenters, and commercial towers, switching seamlessly between utility power and backup generators without manual intervention.

  • Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) mechanisms
  • Engine monitoring & battery charge control
  • Fail-safe interlocks and emergency stop logic

CT Meter Cubicles & Commercial Distribution

Utility-approved Current Transformer (CT) metering cubicles and multi-tenant power distribution networks built for high-density residential towers and shopping complexes.

  • Tamper-proof metering compartments
  • Accurate energy measurement modules
  • Independent tenant circuit isolation

Home Electrical Panel Boards

High-grade domestic distribution boards offering full home protection, RCBO residual current sensing, lightning surge protection, and clean modular designs matching modern interiors.

  • 3-Phase & Single-Phase configurations
  • Class-tested miniature circuit breakers (MCBs)
  • Shock-proof, fire-retardant enclosures

APFC & Power Quality Solutions

Automatic Power Factor Correction capacitor banks maintaining unity power factor, eliminating utility surcharges and optimizing energy distribution across industrial plants.

  • Microprocessor-based power factor relays
  • Heavy-duty detuned harmonic filter reactors
  • Low-loss, self-healing power capacitors
